To set an upper bound on the reactive power injection or absorption of a unit,
the [unit\_capacity\_reactive](@ref) needs to be defined on a [unit\_\_to\_node](@ref) (injection)
or [node\_\_to\_unit](@ref) (absorption) relationship. By defining the parameter, the [unit\_flow\_reactive](@ref var_unit_flow_reactive) variables
to or from a [node](@ref) or a group of nodes will be constrained.
