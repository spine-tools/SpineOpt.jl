The discount rate used to discount future costs to the [discount\_year](@ref).

It is only used when [multiyear\_economic\_discounting](@ref) is set. Then investment costs are converted to discounted annuities,
and operating costs are multiplied by discounted durations. The default is 0, i.e. no discounting.
