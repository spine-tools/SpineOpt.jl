The lead time of a storage investment, i.e. the time between the investment decision and the start of operation.
It works like [lead\_time](@ref) for units and connections: it only affects the economic representation of the investment costs
when [multiyear\_economic\_discounting](@ref) is set, and does not delay the availability of the invested storage capacity.

The default is 0. See [Multi-year investments](@ref).
